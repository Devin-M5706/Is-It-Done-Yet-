# Catering to the criteria — without breaking the idea

The goal is **not** to bolt features onto the project until every box is ticked. That fragments the story and judges smell it. The goal is to notice that the four criteria are *already inside* the core idea, and to strengthen the idea in the exact directions that happen to score.

---

## The filter (use this on every idea)

> **The coherence test:** Does this make *action-free, embodied co-regulation* **more true**?
> - **Yes** → build it. It scores *because* it deepens the idea.
> - **No, but it scores points** → it's track-gaming. It dilutes the story. **Cut it.**

Every suggestion below passes that test. The "avoid" lists are the things that fail it.

---

## The narrative spine (one story that answers all four criteria)

> "In a panic attack, your executive function collapses — you *can't* unlock a phone, find an app, and tap through screens. Every mental-health tool demands the exact actions you can least perform. So we built the first one that demands **nothing**. It notices on its own, and it settles you the way another person would — through touch, through rhythm, below language. No app. No button. No words."

Tell *that* story, and each criterion falls out of it naturally:

| Criterion | Where it already lives in the idea |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|
| ⭐ **WOW** | "It needs nothing from you" + a plush that responds in your hands = the jaw-drop |
| 🧠 **Technical** | To make "needs nothing" *real*, you're forced into hard passive sensing + real-time ML + multi-device orchestration |
| 💡 **Originality** | The inversion (passive vs. every app that demands action) + embodiment is the novelty |
| 🎨 **Design** | The *absence* of a UI is the design decision — the best interface for panic is no interface |

You don't add anything to hit these. You just build the idea well and *say it right*.

---

## ⭐ WOW — amplify the idea, don't add spectacle

**Fits the story:**
- **Lead with the silence.** Open the demo with the *passive auto-trigger* — the judge does nothing and the system reacts. "It knew before I said anything" is the WOW, and it's literally the thesis.
- **Make the judge the user.** Hand them the wristband + plush. The WOW is *their* body being noticed and settled — not your slide.
- **Choreograph mirror → lead.** The plush quickens *with* them, then breathes *slower* than them and leads them down. That single beat is the whole project, felt.

**Would be gaming (avoid):** flashy LED shows, a giant animated dashboard, a big number counter. That's **Visual Appeal — an explicit NON-criterion.** Spectacle ≠ WOW.

---

## 🧠 Technical — the depth the idea *requires* (not bolted on)

Everything here exists because the idea can't be true without it. That's what makes it cohesive.

**Fits the story:**
- **Personalization / baseline calibration.** Everyone's panic signature differs — a fixed threshold is a *lie*. A 30-second on-wear baseline that adapts per person is *required* for passive detection to actually work. (Depth **and** "learn something new.")
- **Sensor fusion to close the freezing-panic blind spot.** Motion misses panic that presents as stillness. Adding a second signal (heart rate via PPG) makes "it notices" *more true* — depth that serves the idea, not decorates it.
- **On-device / edge inference.** A wearable reading your physiology should be low-power and private — process on-body, don't ship raw physiological data to a cloud. Real technical work that *also* becomes a design/trust point (see below).
- **Persistence + hysteresis + honest "arousal signature ≠ diagnosis."** Rigor that directly serves not-crying-wolf. Judges reward teams who know their system's limits.

**Would be gaming (avoid):** a giant deep net where an interpretable Random Forest is the *correct* edge choice; blockchain; an LLM sprinkled where it adds nothing. Complexity for its own sake is the *opposite* of technical ability.

---

## 💡 Originality — protect the real novelty

**Fits the story:**
- **Say the anti-pitch:** "This is not another anxiety app — there's no app to open. That's the entire point." Preempt the pattern-match.
- **Frame novelty as mechanism, not topic:** *passive + embodied co-regulation*, mirror → lead. The idea is a device that behaves like a *relationship*.

**Would be gaming (avoid):** leaning on "mental health matters" as the hook. The topic is crowded, and **practicality is a NON-criterion.** Your novelty is the *how*, never the *why-it-matters*.

---

## 🎨 Design — the invisible interaction *is* the design

Design here means intuitive + engaging for the target audience — and the target audience is someone mid-panic who can't use an interface.

**Fits the story:**
- **Design the felt interaction deliberately:** the 4-in / 6-out cadence, the plush's softness and warmth (C-tactile — functional, not cute), the ritual of putting it on. Be able to explain every choice.
- **Build the relationship in calm moments.** You hold the plush when you're *fine*, so it's trusted when you're not. That's a genuine UX insight for this audience.
- **Reframe the dashboard as the observer/clinician view** — not the user's screen. "No user UI" becomes a thesis, not a gap.
- **Privacy as design.** "Your physiological data never leaves your body." For this audience that's *felt* trust — and it's the same decision as on-device inference. **One choice, two criteria.**

**Would be gaming (avoid):** a polished landing page, slick app screens, pretty gradients. That's **Visual Appeal — the NON-criterion.** Spend the hours on the *interaction*, not the pixels.

---

## The double-fits — where coherence pays off

The strongest moves each serve *multiple* criteria from *one* authentic decision. This is the opposite of gaming:

| Build this | Because it's true to the idea | Criteria it serves at once |
|------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Per-person calibration | detection is a lie without it | Technical + Design (trust) |
| Sensor fusion (PPG) for freezing panic | "it notices" must mean *always* | Technical + Originality |
| On-device processing | your body's data stays on your body | Technical + Design (privacy/trust) |
| Plush mirror → lead choreography | it's the co-regulation mechanism | WOW + Originality + Design |
| Participatory demo (judge = user) | the system acts *on a person* | WOW + Design |

Build these five and you've deepened the idea while naturally covering all four criteria — no box-ticking required.

---

## Net for the sprint

- **Don't add:** landing pages, LED spectacle, gratuitous models, market-size slides. (All fail the coherence test; most are literally non-criteria.)
- **Do deepen:** calibration, the freezing-panic second signal, on-device/privacy, the mirror→lead plush, the participatory demo.
- **Say it as one story:** the thing that needs nothing from you. Every criterion is a facet of that sentence.
